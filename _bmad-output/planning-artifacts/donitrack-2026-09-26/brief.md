# Product Brief: MinanTrack

## Executive Summary

MinanTrack is a simple, reliable tracking platform for people sending and receiving parcels across borders, especially for diaspora families and small logistics actors. The product turns fragmented shipment updates into one clear place: where a parcel is, when it is expected, what the current status means, and what action is needed next.

The problem today is not a lack of shipping services; it is the lack of visibility. Customers often receive updates from multiple channels, lose track of the shipment timeline, and cannot clearly know whether a parcel is delayed, at customs, in transit, or ready for pickup. MinanTrack brings that visibility into a single workflow that feels understandable for both end users and operators.

This first version focuses on the essentials: parcel tracking, delivery status updates, route visibility, pricing estimates, and a clean operational dashboard for agents and administrators. It is designed to serve a real user need in a fast-moving logistics environment while staying lightweight enough to launch quickly and iterate with evidence.

## The Problem

International parcel sending is often stressful and opaque. Customers do not know:

- whether the parcel has actually been picked up;
- whether it has cleared a checkpoint or customs;
- whether delivery is delayed or stuck in the last-mile stage;
- what the cost, timeline, or available delivery options are.

This problem is amplified for diaspora customers who often coordinate shipments across countries, rely on informal or local transport agents, and need confidence that a parcel is progressing as expected. Existing experiences are often fragmented across WhatsApp, SMS, local agents, and carrier portals, which makes it hard to trust the information and act quickly when something goes wrong.

For logistics operators, the cost of confusion is also high: a shipment that lacks visible status creates repeated support requests, missed deliveries, and poor customer satisfaction. The status quo forces teams to handle issues manually, often with incomplete information and no single source of truth.

## The Solution

MinanTrack gives users a clear parcel lifecycle from dispatch to final delivery. The system tracks shipments, updates their current stage, highlights delays and exceptions, and gives the user a simple interpretation of what the status means. It also gives operators a practical dashboard to manage parcels, follow transporter routes, review customer communications, and resolve issues in one place.

The platform is built around a few essential capabilities:

- parcel registration and tracking;
- delivery status timeline with milestones;
- transporter and route management;
- pricing and quote estimation;
- customer notifications and updates;
- operational alerts for delays or exceptions;
- administrative visibility for teams and support.

The experience stays mobile-first and easy to understand, because the user’s main need is confidence and clarity, not technical complexity.

## What Makes This Different

MinanTrack is designed specifically for delivery flows where trust, visibility, and coordination matter more than advanced billing features. This is not a generic parcel app; it is a practical operating layer for logistics journeys that are cross-border, often informal, and highly dependent on local courier coordination.

The product differentiates itself by combining three things:

- a simple user experience for non-technical customers;
- operational visibility for transport agents and back-office teams;
- a strong emphasis on status clarity, exceptions, and delivery confidence.

This matters because most users do not need a complex logistics system — they need a dependable view of their shipment and a way to act when something changes. MinanTrack focuses on that outcome first.

## Who This Serves

### Primary Users

- Senders of parcels to family, friends, or business partners in another country;
- Recipients who need to know when a parcel will arrive and what is required to collect it;
- Local transport agents or couriers managing handoffs and route stages;
- Logistics coordinators or support staff needing a real-time operational view.

### Secondary Users

- Small logistics businesses and dispatch teams;
- Cross-border shipping partners and transport networks;
- Administrators overseeing delivery performance and exception handling.

### Core Jobs to Be Done

- “I want to know where my parcel is right now.”
- “I need to estimate the cost and expected delivery window before sending.”
- “I need to coordinate driver and pickup handoffs without confusion.”
- “I need to spot delays early and respond before the customer asks.”

## Success Criteria

We will consider MinanTrack successful when it meaningfully reduces uncertainty in shipment follow-up and improves the operational speed of teams managing deliveries.

### User Success Signals

- customers can track a parcel without contacting support;
- users understand the current parcel status from a quick glance;
- delivery exceptions are visible before the parcel becomes a problem;
- senders and recipients trust the timeline and estimate.

### Business Success Signals

- fewer manual status inquiries from customers;
- fewer missed or delayed handoffs for logistics operators;
- increased confidence in the shipment process across multiple agents and stages;
- faster resolution of parcel exceptions through a centralized status view.

## Scope

### In Scope for v1

- parcel creation and identification;
- shipment status timeline from pickup to delivery;
- delivery milestone tracking and exception visibility;
- transport agent and route assignment;
- pricing estimate and validation flow;
- user and admin dashboard experiences;
- notification updates for significant shipment events.

### Out of Scope for v1

- advanced fulfillment automation across every carrier type;
- full ERP or accounting integration;
- sophisticated AI-based route optimization;
- marketplace-style listings or consumer marketplace features;
- multi-tenant enterprise billing and custom role matrix beyond basic admin roles.

This version is intentionally focused on operational clarity and trust rather than a broad expansion into adjacent logistics capabilities.

## Vision

If MinanTrack succeeds, it becomes the default operational layer for regional parcel coordination: simple enough for individual senders, powerful enough for small logistics teams, and clear enough to build trust at every delivery stage. Over time, the platform can expand into more advanced delivery orchestration, partner integrations, and better forecasting without losing its core promise: users should always know where the parcel is, what is happening, and what comes next.
